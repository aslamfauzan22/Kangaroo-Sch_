A Journey of Growth & Discovery

Toddler Transition Programme (TTP) 18 Months-2 Years

Builds Security and Belonging

The Toddler Transition Programme provides a warm, nurturing environment where children develop a strong sense of security, belonging, and confidence as they take their first steps towards greater independence.

Through sensory play, movement, music, exploration, and responsive interactions, toddlers build early communication, social, cognitive, and motor skills while developing self-help abilities and positive relationships with others. Our play-based approach encourages curiosity, resilience, and a love for learning, supporting each child in becoming a confident learner and ensuring a smooth, successful transition into Preschool Year 1.

Playschool (PSY 1)
2-3 Years

Builds Trust and Independence

At this stage, children are taking their first steps away from familiar caregivers and into a broader social environment. Through nurturing relationships, predictable routines, and opportunities for exploration, they begin to develop confidence in themselves and the world around them.

Learning is centered on sensory experiences, movement, communication, and play, helping children gain a sense of autonomy while feeling secure and supported.

Nursery (PSY 2)
3-4 Years

Sparks Curiosity and Confidence

As children become more independent, they are eager to investigate, ask questions, and make choices. The curriculum encourages active participation, imaginative play, and meaningful interactions that allow children to express their ideas and emotions.

Through guided exploration, they learn to take initiative, develop social skills, and build confidence in their ability to engage with others and their environment.

Junior KG (PSY 3)
4-5 Years

Develops Purpose and Problem-Solving

Children at this stage are increasingly capable of planning, creating, and collaborating. They enjoy taking on challenges, experimenting with new ideas, and participating in group experiences.

Learning opportunities are designed to foster creativity, critical thinking, and resilience, helping children understand that effort leads to growth. Positive reinforcement and opportunities for leadership nurture a strong sense of purpose and competence.

Senior KG (PSY 4)
5-6 Years

Builds Competence and School Readiness

In their final preschool year, children consolidate foundational literacy, numeracy, and social-emotional skills while preparing for the transition to primary school. They are encouraged to work independently, persevere through challenges, and take pride in their accomplishments.

Through collaborative projects, inquiry-based learning, and reflective practices, children develop self-confidence, responsibility, and a positive attitude towards learning that supports future academic success.

The Kangaroo Kids Approach

We are a school that holds Maldivian culture and values at its heart. Dhivehi language, Islamic studies, and Quran are integral parts of our curriculum, taught by specialist staff and woven naturally into the life of the school. We believe a strong cultural identity and a global outlook are not in conflict. They strengthen each other.

Our Approach to Learning

Fluidic Learning Methodology

Every child learns differently. Our Fluidic Learning Methodology adapts to each learner’s pace, strengths and interests, creating personalised learning experiences that build essential skills such as Communication, Personal Awareness and a Global Mindset through subjects like Enrichment, Math and Language.

Visible Learning Assessment

Learning becomes more meaningful when progress can be seen and celebrated. Our Visible Learning Assessment approach makes growth visible, encouraging children to develop Creativity, Innovation & Entrepreneurship, and Collaboration both within and beyond the classroom.

Extension of Learning

The most powerful learning happens when children connect ideas to the world around them. Our Extension of Learning approach extends Math and STEAM concepts beyond the classroom, fostering Digital Literacy, Adaptability & Flexibility, and real-world problem-solving skills.
