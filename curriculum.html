15 Habits of the Mind

We have identified 15 Habits of the Mind that are developmentally appropriate for young children.

We believe that introducing these habits from as early as two years of age through intentional practice and focused guidance helps children develop as thoughtful learners who can adapt, grow and thrive amidst constant change. Adapted from Arthur Costa’s work Developing Minds.

What Makes Us Different

Enrichment Programme

Learners participate in modules such as service learning, makerspace, project-based learning, coding and genius hour. Aligned with their interests, these experiences encourage deeper engagement and a richer understanding of concepts.

STEAM Theme Programme

Core concepts and themes are revisited throughout each stage of learning, allowing children to explore them through broader, deeper and increasingly complex experiences over time.

Language Programme

We follow a whole language approach that provides learners with opportunities to acquire, understand, interpret and communicate information effectively, helping them grow into confident communicators.

Math Programme

Through the ELPS (Experience-Language-Picture-Symbol) approach, learners develop mathematical understanding by connecting concepts to real-world contexts and experiences across disciplines.

Creativity Programme

This programme encourages learners to develop a wide range of skills through sensory exploration, creative experimentation with art materials, and investigation of processes, concepts and ideas.

Delivering through Fluidic Learning Methodology

Our Learning System is designed to remain agile and responsive to the ever-changing world children are growing up in. Recognising that every learner is unique, the Fluidic Learning Methodology provides flexibility in what children learn, how they learn and when they learn. It enables a seamless integration of digital and physical learning experiences while bringing together curriculum goals, learning stimuli, facilitators, individual needs, attitudes and innate strengths into a cohesive learning journey.

What Children Learn

Dhivehi, Islamic Studies, and Quran

We are a school that holds Maldivian culture and values at its heart. Dhivehi language, Islamic studies, and Quran are integral parts of our curriculum, taught by specialist staff and woven naturally into the life of the school. We believe a strong cultural identity and a global outlook are not in conflict. They strengthen each other.

Language & Literacy

We use a multi-sensory, phonics-based reading programme that develops children’s reading, writing, and communication skills in English.

Children experience language through stories, songs, conversation, drama, and hands-on activities that make literacy feel alive and purposeful.

Dhivehi language development runs alongside this, ensuring children grow into confident communicators in both languages.

Mathematics

Mathematics at Kangaroo Kids is hands-on and discovery-based. Children explore number, pattern, shape, and measurement through physical materials, problem solving activities, and real-world contexts that make abstract concepts accessible and meaningful. We want children to feel confident and curious about mathematics, not anxious or passive.

Science & the World

From the earliest stages, we nurture children’s natural curiosity about the world around them. Science is taught through investigation, observation, and questioning, with children conducting simple experiments, making predictions, and drawing their own conclusions. We are building the habits of a scientist alongside genuine scientific knowledge.

The Arts

Creative expression is central to who we are. Children explore visual art, and movement throughout the week, developing confidence, imagination, and the ability to communicate ideas in many different forms. The arts also play a vital role in emotional development, giving children a language for feelings and experiences they are still finding words for.
