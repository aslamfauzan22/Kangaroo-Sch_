How to Apply to Kangaroo Kids

Joining the Kangaroo Kids community is a clear three-step process. We have designed it to be straightforward and welcoming, giving you and your child the opportunity to learn about us while we learn about you. We want every family to feel confident and well-informed from the very beginning.

Complete your Online Application

Begin by filling out our online enquiry form. We will ask for some basic information about your child, their age, and your preferred session times. You are also welcome to tell us a little about your child’s personality, their interests, and any specific needs they may have. The more we know from the start, the better prepared we will be to welcome your child on their first day.

Once we receive your form, our admissions team will be in touch within two working days to confirm receipt and discuss next steps.

Screening & Welcome Meeting

We invite every prospective family for a brief screening visit to our Dream Campus. This is a relaxed, informal session where your child can explore the school environment while you meet with a member of our leadership team. For some year groups, this includes a short parent-child activity to help us understand your child’s current stage of development.

This visit is not a test, and there is no pass or fail. It is simply a chance for us to begin a relationship and to make sure Kangaroo Kids is the right fit for your family. We encourage you to ask questions, look around the classrooms, and get a feel for the school. Many families tell us that this visit is the moment they knew Kangaroo Kids was the right choice.

For families who are not yet in the Maldives, we are happy to arrange a virtual tour and video call with our admissions team as an alternative.

Confirm Your Place

Once the screening is complete, we will confirm your child’s place in writing. To secure the place, you will be asked to:

  • Pay the one-time registration fee of MVR 2,000
  • Complete and sign the school enrolment documentation
  • Confirm your preferred session time

Your child’s session start date will then be agreed with the admissions team. We will also share information about what to expect on the first day, how our settling-in process works, and how we will keep you updated on your child’s progress throughout the year.

Documents Required

To complete enrolment, please have the following ready:

  • Child’s birth certificate
  • Parent or guardian’s national ID or passport
  • Recent passport-sized photograph of your child
  • Any relevant medical or dietary information

If you have any questions about the documentation required, our admissions team is happy to help.

Sibling Discount

We are pleased to offer a discount for families with more than one child enroled at Kangaroo Kids:

  • 10% discount on monthly fee when two siblings are enroled simultaneously
  • 20% discount on monthly fee when three or more siblings are enroled simultaneously

These discounts are applied automatically once both or all siblings are formally enroled and are reflected in your monthly invoice. We believe that keeping siblings together at the same school benefits the whole family, and our discount is our way of making that easier.

Payment Terms

Monthly fee are due before the 15th of each calendar month. Families with outstanding fee balances at the close of the Academic Year will have year-end transcripts withheld until all Outstanding Fee have been paid in full.

We understand that circumstances can change. If you are experiencing financial difficulty, we encourage you to speak with our admissions team in confidence. We will always do our best to work with families where we can.

What Is Included in the Fee?

Monthly tuition fee cover:

  • Full access to the Kangaroo Kids KKEL curriculum and all learning materials.
  • Specialist teaching in Dhivehi, Islam and Quran.
  • 2 sets of uniforms per academic year, all books and stationeries. All included in the monthly fee. Students are only required to bring their snack boxes and water bottles to school.
  • Use of all campus facilities, including our purpose-designed playground.
  • Regular progress updates and parent communications throughout the year.

Full details of any optional extras are available from the admissions team before you enrol, so there are no surprises.

An Investment In Their Future

We know that choosing an independent school is a significant financial commitment, and we appreciate the trust families place in us when they choose Kangaroo Kids. We are committed to offering outstanding value: a world-class early years education, a purpose-built campus, a dedicated and experienced teaching team, and a community that supports your child and your family throughout their time with us.

If you have any questions about fee, payment options, or what is included in the programme, please do not hesitate to contact our admissions team.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for admission?

Simply complete the online application form through our website on Skolaro. Once we receive your application, our Admissions Team will contact you to arrange an admission meeting with you and your child.

What happens during the admission meeting?

The admissions meeting is a relaxed and friendly session where we get to know your child and your family. It also gives you the opportunity to learn more about our programmes, teaching approach, and the school environment.

Can I apply if we are relocating from abroad?

Yes. We welcome families relocating to the Maldives and can guide you through the admissions process remotely where needed. Our Admissions Team is happy to arrange a virtual meeting and answer your questions by email or phone before you arrive. We have supported many international families through this process and understand the unique challenges of relocating.

What if there are no places available?

If your preferred programme or year group is full, we will add your child to our waiting list and contact you as soon as a place becomes available. Places do become available throughout the year, so we encourage you to get in touch even if you have heard that availability is limited.

Can siblings apply together?

Yes. We encourage siblings to join our Kangaroo Kids family and offer a 10% sibling discount on monthly tuition fee for the younger child. Many families also find that attending school together helps children settle in more quickly and makes their school experience even more enjoyable.

What is included in the school fee?

Our fee are designed to be all-inclusive, giving parents peace of mind and making school life simple and convenient. School fee include all books, learning materials, classroom resources, and two sets of school uniforms each academic year. Children only need to bring a water bottle and a healthy snack each day, we take care of everything else so they can focus on learning, playing, and growing.

How does Kangaroo Kids support children with different learning needs?

Every child learns at their own pace. Our teachers carefully observe each child's progress and work closely with families to provide the support they need. Where appropriate, personalised learning plans are developed in partnership with parents to help every child thrive. Our SEN Coordinator, School Counsellor and Early Intervention Teachers also provide additional support whenever required.

Does the school provide counselling and wellbeing support?

Yes. Our qualified School Counsellor supports children's social, emotional, and behavioural wellbeing. We work closely with parents and teachers to help children build confidence, develop positive relationships, and enjoy a happy school experience. Our curriculum further supports children's emotional growth and personal development.

What if my child finds it difficult to settle into school?

It's perfectly normal for young children to need time to adjust. Our experienced teachers and School Counsellor work closely with families to help children settle into their new routine at their own pace, ensuring they feel safe, secure, and confident in their new environment.

How will I know about my child's progress?

We believe that strong partnerships with parents are essential. You'll receive regular updates through parent-teacher meetings, and ongoing communication with your child's teachers, keeping you informed about their learning and development every step of the way. We use a mobile application called Class Dojo as our main mode of communication with parents where teachers share all important notices, updates, information and also photos and videos of daily lessons and special events at the school.

Still have a question?

We're always happy to help. If you have any questions about admissions, our programmes, or whether Kangaroo Kids is the right fit for your child, please get in touch with our Admissions Team. We'd love to welcome you for a visit and show you what makes Kangaroo Kids such a special place to learn and grow.
