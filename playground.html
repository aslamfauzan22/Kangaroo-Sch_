Time to run, climb, and explore!

Play is not a break from learning. Play is how young children learn.

Our playground at Kangaroo Kids has been thoughtfully designed to support every dimension of physical and cognitive development, providing a space where children feel free to explore, take managed risks, and build the physical foundations for a healthy, active life.

Designed for
Preschool Children

Our playground is not a generic outdoor space bolted onto the side of a building. It has been specifically designed for children aged 18 months to six years, with each element carefully chosen to reflect the developmental needs and physical capabilities of each age group.

Every piece of equipment, every surface, and every space has been selected with purpose: to challenge children appropriately, to keep them safe, and to make outdoor time genuinely enriching rather than simply recreational. We have invested in our outdoor environment because we know that what happens outside the classroom is just as important as what happens inside it.

What Children Develop

Gross Motor Skills

Running, climbing, jumping, and balancing develop large muscle groups and whole-body coordination.

Balance and Vestibular Development

The vestibular system underpins a child’s sense of balance, spatial awareness, and body control.

Tactile Development

Touch is vital in early childhood. Our playground features varied textures and surfaces to stimulate tactile development and healthy sensory integration.

Social and Emotional Learning

The playground is a key space for social learning. Taking turns, cooperating, and resolving conflicts help children build empathy, communication, and resilience.

Safe and Supervised

Child safety is our top priority. Our playground is always supervised by trained staff who balance fun challenges with complete safety.

Outdoors Every Day

We believe children need outdoor time every single day, and we protect that time in our daily schedule. Time outdoors is not a reward or an optional extra.

Enrichment Programmes

Beyond the core curriculum, Kangaroo Kids offers a rich range of enrichment programmes that broaden children's experiences and deepen their skills.

The Splash Programme

Swimming is an essential life skill. Our signature SPLASH Programme provides free, structured swimming lessons led by trained instructors, helping children build water safety, confidence, and fitness.

Caring Hands Club

Our health, hygiene, and wellbeing initiative builds lifelong habits through engaging activities from handwashing to emotional awareness. Annually, Health Education Week brings doctors and experts to the school to share their expertise.

The STAR Programme

The STAR Programme is the youngest level of the Girl Guide programme, helping girls build confidence, leadership, and essential life skills through engaging activities and character-building experiences.

The Pre-Cub Programme

It is the introductory level of Scouting for young children at Kangaroo Kids. Through fun, age-appropriate activities, it develops independence, teamwork, responsibility, and a spirit of adventure.
Outdoor experiences, community projects, creative challenges, and leadership opportunities help children build confidence and resilience.
